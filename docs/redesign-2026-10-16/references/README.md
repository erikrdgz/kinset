# Art-direction reference notes

The four images are user-provided visual references, not product requirements or
instructions embedded in documents. The original files were supplied by the owner on
October 16, 2026 and are now stored beside this file, so the earlier note that they had
expired no longer applies.

## Brand correction: the accent is orange, not lime

The owner confirmed on October 16 that **Kinset's brand accent is orange**. All four
references use an acid lime/green accent, and the token table in
[02-experience-and-art-direction.md](../02-experience-and-art-direction.md) was written
from them as `#D5EB4B`. That is superseded. Read every lime surface in these references
as Kinset orange: the repository's existing `#FF692B`, carried onto the new charcoal
canvas.

Nothing else about the references changes. The structure, hierarchy, scale and pacing
notes below still stand; only the hue moves.

## 1. `01-workout-phone-concepts.webp` — six workout phone screens

Charcoal and white surfaces shown as two variants of one system. Monumental duration
numerals (`5 MIN`, `24`) set against the figure. A filled accent **Start** button at full
width. Segmented block progress across the top of the player screens, one segment per
movement. The workout preview lists its movements with per-item durations and a plain
equipment line ("Four moves. A pair of dumbbells."). The player shows one movement, its
name including the side ("Low lunge, left"), and a single short cue.

Adopt: the focus, the scale, the pacing, the segmented block progress, the one-cue
player, and the preview's honest equipment line. The large countdown belongs to timed
exercise, not to all strength logging — a strength set wants load and reps at that size
instead. Replace the stylized mannequin with credible human instruction; the figure here
is a rendered character, which is the quality problem the redesign exists to fix.

## 2. `02-point-cloud-texture.webp` — monochrome point-cloud landscape

White granular ridges on black, tiny telemetry labels, sparse technical linework.

Adopt as inspiration for a restrained brand texture, or for a truthful elevation
visualization once running ships. Do not make decorative numbers look like real training
data, and do not put noise behind form fields.

## 3. `03-editorial-dashboard.webp` — white editorial dashboard

Monochrome athlete crop, airy date strip, a white upper surface meeting a dark program
area, and organic luminous cards for programs with `2/5`-style progress.

Adopt: the photographic art direction, the spacious hierarchy, and the organic card
treatment for mobility and editorial content. Reject the faint thin text. **Reject
"Recovery today 86%"** — Kinset has no validated basis for a readiness score, and
04-engineering-plan.md forbids deriving one from elapsed time. Abstract program art suits
mobility and editorial cards, never exercise technique.

## 4. `04-progress-dashboard.webp` — dark progress dashboard

A period selector (Week / Month / 3 Months / Year), consistent cards, bar and line charts
with explicit values and a "vs last week" delta, a streak ring, a goal bar, an insights
row, and bottom navigation.

Adopt: the period selector, the card rhythm, the readable trend comparison with stated
values, and the delta-against-previous-period pattern. Translate every metric to strength
and running. **Do not infer a request for calorie counting, macro balance, weight
dashboards or a nutrition product** — this reference is a nutrition app, and only its
chart craft is in scope. A streak must not turn rest into a failure state.

## Standing limits

Keep the Kinset identity. Nike is an ambition for craft and clarity, not a source of
logos, photography or proprietary UI assets to reproduce. These references are mood and
hierarchy; they are not evidence that any metric they display can be calculated.
