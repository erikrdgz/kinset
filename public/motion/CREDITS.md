# Kinset movement previews

Character mesh and rig: Mesh2Motion human-base-animations.glb, provided under CC0.
Primary source: https://github.com/Mesh2Motion/mesh2motion-app
Source assets: https://github.com/Mesh2Motion/mesh2motion-assets
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Downloaded through the CC0 mirror in https://github.com/AssiamahS/opengym3d/tree/main/motions/cc0/mesh2motion

Kinset's trainer.glb retains the character mesh and rig only; texture and animation data were removed, and the material was changed to graphite. Kinset's 30 exercise motions are authored procedurally in src/motion/pose.ts; it is not motion capture from a trainer and is not a professionally reviewed technique demonstration. Review is required before these previews are used as instructional content in a release.

Dumbbell, bench, mat, and machine geometry and playback controls are authored for Kinset. No Mixamo animations are included.
