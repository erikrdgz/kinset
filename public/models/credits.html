How Kinset uses these models

The GLB files are redistributed unchanged. Kinset changes materials at runtime and highlights selected muscle names. Model-based visual adaptations remain under CC BY-SA 4.0; this asset license does not imply endorsement by the source projects.

Model files are publicly available: muscle model and skeleton model.

This anatomical visualization is for general exercise education. Exercise-to-muscle mappings are broad and have not been clinically validated.
